FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to get started?

Just an internet connection and any phone, tablet, or laptop you already have. No special equipment is required.

How do customers pay?

At your physical shop counter, staff can accept Cash or UPI. When customers order online from their phone, they pay using UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay) or their pre-loaded Store Wallet.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

When you sign up, your store is automatically on the 14-day free trial. You can test all features with zero payment upfront.
